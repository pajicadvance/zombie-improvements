Integrated features from [Leader Zombie Tweaks](https://modrinth.com/mod/leader-zombie-tweaks) by ThePotatoArchivist into the mod:
- Added option "Increased leader damage": Slightly increases damage dealt by leader zombies. Enabled by default. Damage amount is configurable.
- Added option "Leader size increase": Increases the size of leader zombies. Default incrase is 0.125.
- Added option "Unique texture for leaders": Gives leader zombies a unique texture. Enabled by default.
- Added option "Unique sounds for leaders": Gives leader zombies unique sounds. Enabled by default.
- Added option "Add 'Leader' prefix to name": Appends a "Leader" prefix to the name of the leader zombie. Enabled by default.
